\caption{\textbf{Contact-force-constrained control trades goal accuracy for bounded contact force.}
(\textbf{A}) Matched views of the two-segment, tendon-actuated PCS arm at the
last saved instant, $t=7.999$\,s: nominal HOCLF control (left) and
HOCLF--HOCBF control (right). Both show the current recorded configuration
with the same camera, scale, coral material, and dark mount. The rounded
tip represents the spherical contact geometry at the terminal backbone point. Purple spheres
are obstacles. A white dot marks the current end-effector position at the
terminal backbone point; a green ring marks its target. The dashed connector
shows their projected separation, and both markers remain visible through
the robot.
(\textbf{B}) Maximum pairwise normal contact force over the saved rollout.
The nominal controller reaches $33.61$\,N, while the constrained controller
remains below the prescribed $5$\,N limit, with a maximum of $4.987$\,N.
(\textbf{C}) End-effector distance to the target over the same interval.
The final distances are $0.042$\,mm and $30.06$\,mm for the nominal and
constrained controllers, respectively. Colors and line styles identify the
controllers consistently in both plots. These results describe the evaluated
simulation rollout and its maximum pairwise normal-force metric.}
