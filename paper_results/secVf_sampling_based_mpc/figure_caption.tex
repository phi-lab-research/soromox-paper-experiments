\caption{\textbf{Contact-rich manipulation with a Franka-mounted soft tentacle.}
(\textbf{A}) The complete system during an iCEM-controlled push. The Franka
is rendered to represent the kinematically prescribed tentacle-base motion;
its dynamics are not part of the experiment. The soft tentacle is coral, the
manipulated box is gold, and the active goal and desired path are green.
(\textbf{B}) Matched detail views at $t=0.60$ and $0.95$\,s show the tentacle
redirecting the box after the first corner. All renderings use the saved
iCEM seed-0 trajectory.
(\textbf{C}) Object trajectories for three seeds each of iCEM
($K=200,100,50$ over three iterations) and MPPI ($K=800$).
Colors identify methods and solid, dashed, and dotted lines identify seeds
0, 1, and 2. The circle marks the start, crosses the final positions, and
numbered green squares the ordered goals. iCEM completes all four goals in
3/3 runs, versus 1/3 for MPPI. Planning time is the mean and standard deviation
across the three per-seed averages, excluding each run's first MPC step:
$26.5\pm0.2$\,s for iCEM and $8.5\pm0.8$\,s for MPPI.
Planning is slower than real time.}
