\caption{\textbf{Sampling-based contact-rich manipulation with a soft tentacle.}
(\textbf{A}) The complete Franka--tentacle system during an iCEM-controlled
push at $t=0.95$\,s. The Franka represents the kinematically prescribed
motion of the tentacle base; its dynamics are not part of the experiment.
The soft tentacle is coral, the manipulated box is gold, and the active goal
and desired path are green. Faint complete-system poses at $t=1.05$ and
$1.15$\,s show subsequent recorded motion toward the same highlighted goal.
The cube closes from $83$ to $41$ to $15$\,mm from that goal. These are later
executed states, rather than MPC predictions; the rigid-arm poses follow the
recorded tentacle-base poses kinematically.
(\textbf{B}) Object trajectories for three seeds each of iCEM
($K=200,100,50$ over three iterations) and MPPI ($K=800$).
Colors identify methods and solid, dashed, and dotted lines identify seeds
0, 1, and 2. The circle marks the start, crosses the final positions, and
numbered green squares the ordered goals. iCEM completes all four goals in
3/3 runs, versus 1/3 for MPPI. Planning time is the mean and population standard
deviation across the three per-seed averages, excluding each run's first MPC
step: $26.5\pm0.2$\,s for iCEM and $8.5\pm0.8$\,s for MPPI.
Planning is slower than real time.
(\textbf{C}) Left to right: three successive iCEM sampling iterations at the
first planning step, with 200, 100, and 50 candidates respectively, showing
candidate configurations at horizon step 10.
Translucent blue configurations show sampled predictions; opacity decreases
with cost rank. The lowest-cost prediction and current configuration use
solid coral for the tentacle and gold for the object. All renderings use
the saved iCEM seed-0 trajectory and the same studio lighting and materials.
The same fixed crop is applied to each sampling view. Iterations 2 and 3
have similar displayed configurations: the selected object positions differ
by only $1.2$\,mm at this prediction step, while the best whole-rollout cost
decreases by $4.6\%$ in the final iteration.
(\textbf{D}) Mean planning wall-clock time and mean negative push cost per
MPC step for the single-goal planner sweep, averaged over three seeds.
Higher negative cost is better. Triangles denote MPPI with varying sample
count; crosses denote iCEM with three iterations and varying sample count;
open circles denote iCEM with $K=200$ and varying iteration count.
The cross and circle for $K=200$ with three iterations coincide.
The shared method colors apply to (B) and (D). This sweep is separate from
the square-push comparison in (B); the appendix identifies each configuration.}
