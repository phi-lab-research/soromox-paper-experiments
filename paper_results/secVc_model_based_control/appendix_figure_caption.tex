\caption{\textbf{Configuration-space control and actuation-space gain optimization.}
(\textbf{A}) Commanded physical strains of a fully actuated one-segment PCS robot under five controllers, with the same model, reference, and feedback gains. These are different initializations. Dashed curves are targets, and shading gives the pointwise coordinate-wise min--max range across all six initial runs.
