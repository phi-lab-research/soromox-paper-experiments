\caption{\textbf{Model-based pose tracking and automatic controller tuning.}
(\textbf{A}) A fully actuated two-segment PCS robot under operational-space impedance control follows a spherical figure-eight pose reference, shown solid at $t=6.25$\,s with its tip facing the reader. Three faint shapes show other recorded configurations at $t=4.0$\,s, $t=4.75$\,s, and $t=7.25$\,s. The green dashed curve is the desired tip path; the green ring, normal, and radial spoke indicate the instantaneous target pose. The complete 10\,s rollout has position and geodesic orientation RMSEs of $2.84$\,mm and $2.29^\circ$, respectively.
(\textbf{B}) Gain tuning for a separate, single-segment, three-tendon PCS robot under synergistic operational-space control. Matched views at $t=0.5$\,s compare the initial run closest to the median initial loss (start 5 of 6) with the lowest-loss optimized run (start 2, iteration 100). These are different initializations. White dots mark the current tip positions; green rings mark their common target. The blue band spans the pointwise minimum and maximum Euclidean position errors across all six initial gain sets; the blue and coral curves show the selected initial and best optimized runs. The errors cover the full 5\,s rollouts; dots identify the displayed instant. Optimizing the gains reduces the objective by $98.8\%$ relative to the initial loss median across all six starts.}
