\caption{\textbf{Batching increases aggregate GPU simulation throughput across four model families.}
(\textbf{A}) Articulated robots, (\textbf{B}) planar PCS, (\textbf{C}) spatial PCS, and (\textbf{D}) GVS, with $N\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32\}$ links or segments. All panels share axis scales and the same encoding of $N$. The simulation-throughput factor $\Gamma_\mathrm{sim}=n_\mathrm{b}T_\mathrm{sim}/T_\mathrm{wall}$ measures total simulated time across the batch per unit wall-clock time; the dotted horizontal line marks aggregate parity ($\Gamma_\mathrm{sim}=1$). Markers show the measured settings and connecting lines guide the eye. Measurements use an RTX 5090 in double precision, with one-second rollouts and the mean of three timed executions after one discarded warm-up. Articulated models use JAX; PCS and GVS use Warp kernels. Full experimental details are given in \Cref{sec:app:gpu_batch_benchmark}.}
