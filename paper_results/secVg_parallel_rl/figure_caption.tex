\caption{\textbf{Parallel reinforcement learning for target tracking.}
(\textbf{A}) All 64 independent environments from a saved rollout of the same
trained policy, shown at $t=5$\,s and $t=15$\,s with a matched camera and scale.
Each coral robot is a single-segment, tendon-actuated PCS arm; its green sphere
is the moving end-effector target. Spatial offsets arrange the environments
in an $8\times8$ grid for display. Mean tracking errors across the 64 shown
environments are $5.20$\,mm and $4.51$\,mm at the two instants, respectively.
(\textbf{B}) Mean episode return versus logged training wall-clock time for
PyElastica and SoRoMoX on two CPU cores, and SoRoMoX on an RTX 4070 with
64--512 parallel environments. Lines show the mean of two runs per setting;
shaded bands show one sample standard deviation. Curves use up to 1,500
records per run, Gaussian smoothing with a scale of 10 records, and
interpolation onto a shared time grid within each setting. Dashed lines
denote CPU runs and solid lines GPU runs. The comparisons include differences
in model formulation, execution backend, hardware, and parallelization.}
